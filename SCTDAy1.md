# Section 01 — 
What Is a Calorie?

A **kilocalorie (kcal)** — what everyone calls a "calorie" — is the amount of energy needed to raise **1 kg of water by 1°C**.

Food labels use kcal. Your body burns kcal.

> 1 food calorie = 1 kilocalorie = 1,000 small calories (physics units)

---

## Energy Sources

### Protein
- 4 kcal per gram
- Builds muscle
- Produces enzymes and hormones

### Carbohydrates
- 4 kcal per gram
- Primary brain fuel
- Fast energy source
- Stored as glycogen

### Fat
- 9 kcal per gram
- Supports hormones
- Builds cell membranes
- Carries fat-soluble vitamins

---

## Energy Balance

### Energy In
- Food
- Drinks
- Alcohol
- Supplements
- Oils

### Energy Out
- BMR (~60–70%)
- NEAT (non-exercise activity)
- Exercise (TEA)
- Thermic Effect of Food (~10%)

### Outcomes

| State | Formula | Result |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Deficit | IN < OUT | Fat Loss |
| Maintenance | IN = OUT | Weight Stable |
| Surplus | IN > OUT | Muscle/Fat Gain |

# Section 02 — Proper Intake

## Fat Loss

**TDEE − 300 to 500 kcal**

- Lose ~0.3–0.5 kg/week
- Preserve muscle with:
  - Protein: 1.6–2.2 g/kg

⚠️ Deficits greater than 750 kcal/day increase risk of:
- Muscle loss
- Hormonal disruption

---

## Maintenance

**Eat at TDEE**

- Weight remains stable
- Ideal for body recomposition
- Works best with:
  - Strength training
  - High protein intake

Most sustainable long-term approach.

---

## Muscle Gain

**TDEE + 200–400 kcal**

- Gain ~0.2–0.4 kg/week
- Minimize fat gain
- Maximize muscle growth

Avoid:
- Dirty bulking
- 1000+ kcal surplus

# Section 03 — Pros & Cons

## Advantages

### ✓ Objective Data
Removes guesswork and provides accurate awareness of intake.

### ✓ Works for Any Goal
Supports fat loss, maintenance, and muscle gain.

### ✓ Builds Food Literacy
Tracking for 8–12 weeks develops long-term intuition.

### ✓ Flexible Dieting
No foods are banned.

### ✓ Macro Awareness
Reveals protein deficiencies and excess fat intake.

### ✓ Evidence-Based
Most successful weight-loss studies use calorie restriction.

---

## Disadvantages

### ✗ Food Labels Can Be Wrong
Errors can reach 20%.

### ✗ May Trigger Obsessive Behavior
Potential issue for individuals with disordered eating history.

### ✗ Ignores Food Quality
200 kcal almonds ≠ 200 kcal soda.

### ✗ Can Be Tedious
Food weighing and logging become burdensome.

### ✗ TDEE Is Only an Estimate
Most formulas have 10–15% error.

### ✗ Metabolic Adaptation
Your metabolism slows during prolonged dieting.

---

## Verdict

Track calories for:

**8–12 weeks**

Then transition toward:

- Approximate tracking
- Intuitive eating
- Periodic check-ins

Goal:

> Learn the system, don't become dependent on it.

# Section 04 — How to Calculate Calories

## Step 1 — Calculate BMR

### Mifflin-St Jeor Formula

#### Men

```text
BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A + 5
```

#### Women

```text
BMR = 10W + 6.25H − 5A − 161
```

Where:

- W = Weight (kg)
- H = Height (cm)
- A = Age (years)

---

### Harris-Benedict Formula

#### Men

```text
BMR = 88.36 + (13.4 × W) + (4.8 × H) − (5.7 × A)
```

#### Women

```text
BMR = 447.6 + (9.2 × W) + (3.1 × H) − (4.3 × A)
```

---

## Step 2 — Calculate TDEE

```text
TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier
```

### Activity Factors

| Activity Level | Multiplier |
|---------------|------------|
| Sedentary | 1.2 |
| Lightly Active | 1.375 |
| Moderately Active | 1.55 |
| Very Active | 1.725 |
| Extra Active | 1.9 |

---

### Goal Adjustments

```text
Fat Loss     = TDEE − 300 to 500
Maintenance  = TDEE
Muscle Gain  = TDEE + 200 to 400
```

# Section 06 — Pro Tips & Hacks

## 1. Weigh Food Raw
Cooking changes water content.

---

## 2. Track Cooking Oils
1 tablespoon oil ≈ 120 kcal.

---

## 3. Use Volume Eating
Fill half your plate with vegetables.

---

## 4. Protein First
Most satiating nutrient.

---

## 5. Beware Liquid Calories

Examples:

- Juice (250ml): 110+ kcal
- Chai with sugar: 60–80 kcal
- Lassi: 200+ kcal

Better options:

- Black coffee
- Black tea
- Lemon water

---

## 6. Recalculate Every 4–6 Weeks
Your TDEE changes as body weight changes.

---

## 7. Use Food Tracking Apps

Recommended:

- Cronometer
- MyFitnessPal
- HealthifyMe

Track consistently for 90 days.

---

## 8. Follow the 80/20 Rule

- 80% whole foods
- 20% flexible foods

Makes adherence easier.
