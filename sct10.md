SLEEP & RECOVERY
Sleep is not passive. It is the most anabolic, restorative, and cognitive-enhancing thing you can do. No supplement, training protocol, or diet replaces it.

😴
WHY SLEEP IS NON-NEGOTIABLE
Testosterone: 60–70% produced during sleep cycles
HGH: Growth hormone peaks in first 3 hrs of sleep
Muscle repair: Protein synthesis happens overnight
Cognition: Memory consolidation, creativity, emotional regulation
Skin: Collagen production + cellular repair overnight
🌙
SLEEP OPTIMIZATION
Same sleep/wake time — 7 days a week, no exceptions
Room temp: 65–68°F (18–20°C) — cooler = deeper sleep
Blackout curtains — zero light exposure in bedroom
No blue light 1hr before — use blue-light glasses or dim warm lights
No caffeine after 1pm — half-life is 5–7 hours
Magnesium 400mg at bed — significantly improves NREM
📊
SLEEP STAGES
N1 (Light): Transition. Don't interrupt this.
N2: Body temp drops. Memory consolidation starts.
N3 (Deep/SWS): Most restorative. HGH release. Physical repair.
REM: Emotional processing, creativity. Testosterone peaks here.
Target: 4–5 cycles (90min each) = 7.5–9hrs ideal
RECOVERY PROTOCOL
🧊
Cold Therapy
2–3 min cold shower daily. Reduces inflammation, boosts dopamine +250%, improves alertness. After training — wait 4hrs to preserve adaptation stimulus.
🔥
Sauna
20 min sessions 2–4×/week if available. Reduces cortisol, improves cardiovascular health, increases HGH. Heat shock proteins = anti-aging.
🧘
Meditation
10–20 min daily. Reduces cortisol, improves emotional regulation, builds present-moment focus. Headspace, Waking Up, or simple breath awareness.
🚶
