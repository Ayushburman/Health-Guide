# Healthy Eating Principles

## ⏰ Right Time

- Eat breakfast within **1–2 hours of waking up**
- Make **lunch (midday meal)** your largest meal
- Keep dinner lighter than lunch
- Finish dinner **2–3 hours before bedtime**
- Maintain **consistent meal times** every day
- Aim for a **12–14 hour overnight fasting gap** between dinner and breakfast

---

## 🍽️ Right Way

- Chew slowly (**20–30 chews per bite**)
- Sit down and eat without screens or distractions
- Stop eating when you feel **80% full**
- Eat **vegetables and protein before carbohydrates**
- Remain seated for **10–15 minutes after meals**

---

## 🥗 Right Item

- Choose **whole foods** over processed foods
- Include **protein, fiber, and healthy fats** in every meal
- Prefer **seasonal fruits and vegetables**
- Choose **whole grains** instead of refined grains
- Limit **added sugar**, sugary drinks, and fried foods

---

## 🎯 Simple Rule

> **Right Time + Right Way + Right Item = Better Digestion, Stable Energy, and Long-Term Health**
