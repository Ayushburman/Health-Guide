09 — Holistic View


## Lifestyle & Skin Health


No amount of product compensates for chronically poor sleep, diet, or stress. The skin is an external mirror of internal health.


#### Sleep


Growth hormone peaks at night, driving collagen repair. Less than 7 hours → elevated cortisol → collagen breakdown, increased inflammation, impaired barrier. 7–9 hours is skin medicine.


#### Diet


High-GI foods → insulin spike → IGF-1 → sebum ↑ → acne. Antioxidant-rich diet (berries, leafy greens, fish) reduces oxidative stress. Collagen peptide supplementation shows modest evidence. Stay hydrated.


#### Stress


Chronic stress → cortisol → reduced ceramide synthesis → leaky barrier → inflammation. Triggers acne, eczema, and psoriasis flares. The skin-brain axis is well-documented. Meditation, exercise, and sleep reduce cortisol.


#### Hydration


Water gets to the skin last (kidneys, brain, muscle first). Use a humidifier in dry climates. External humectants (HA, glycerin) are more reliable than "drinking more water" alone. But dehydration is still real.
