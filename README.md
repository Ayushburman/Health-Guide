# Post-Transplant Recovery Guide · SCT Day +100

# Your New Life Roadmap — After Stem Cell Transplant

A comprehensive guide covering nutrition, hydration, exercise, clothing, sleep, minerals, and mental wellness for life beyond Day +100.

---

## Recovery Timeline

* ✅ Day 0 — Transplant
* ✅ Day +30 — Engraftment
* ✅ Day +60 — Early Recovery
* ⭐ Day +100 — YOU ARE HERE
* ⭕ Day +180 — 6-Month Check
* ⭕ Year 1 — Milestone
* ⭕ Year 2+ — Full Life

---

# ⚕️ Always Consult Your Transplant Team First

This guide is educational and general in nature. Every SCT patient's recovery is unique. All decisions about diet, exercise, medications, and activities must be approved by your hematologist and transplant coordinator.

**Do not make major changes without medical supervision.**

---

# 🥗 Section 01 — Nutrition & Food

## Building Your Immune Army Through Food

## The Post-SCT Plate Method

Designed to rebuild immunity, prevent infection, maintain muscle, and support gut health after your immune system reset.

| Food Group            | Percentage | Examples                         |
| --------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------- |
| Lean Protein          | 30%        | Cooked chicken, eggs, fish, tofu |
| Cooked Vegetables     | 35%        | Steamed/boiled vegetables        |
| Complex Carbohydrates | 25%        | Brown rice, oats, sweet potato   |
| Healthy Fats          | 10%        | Olive oil, avocado, nuts         |

---

## ✅ Safe Foods (Day +100)

* Well-cooked meats, poultry, fish
* Pasteurized dairy
* Steamed or boiled vegetables
* Well-cooked eggs
* Brown rice, oatmeal, bread, pasta
* Peeled fruits
* Olive oil, coconut oil
* Herbal teas
* Homemade soups
* Well-cooked legumes

---

## 🚫 Foods to Avoid

* Raw fish / sushi
* Raw sprouts
* Unpasteurized milk or juice
* Cold deli meats
* Raw or runny eggs
* Food left out > 2 hours
* Raw honey
* Grapefruit
* Unwashed produce
* Moldy cheeses

# 💊 Section 02 — Minerals, Vitamins & Supplements

| Nutrient    | Daily Amount  | Purpose                     |
| ----------- | ------------- | --------------------------- |
| Calcium     | 1000–1200 mg  | Bone health                 |
| Vitamin D3  | 1000–2000 IU  | Immune support              |
| Iron        | Based on labs | Avoid supplementing blindly |
| Magnesium   | 200–400 mg    | Sleep, cramps, fatigue      |
| Zinc        | 8–11 mg       | Immunity                    |
| Vitamin C   | 75–200 mg     | Antioxidant support         |
| Vitamin B12 | 2.4 mcg+      | Nerve & RBC health          |
| Potassium   | Per labs      | Monitor carefully           |
| Folate (B9) | 400 mcg       | DNA repair                  |
| Omega-3     | 1–2 g         | Anti-inflammatory           |
| Selenium    | 55 mcg        | Antioxidant defense         |

---

## ⚠️ Supplement Safety

* Never take herbal supplements without approval
* Avoid high-dose antioxidants
* Avoid:

  * St John’s Wort
  * Echinacea
  * Turmeric supplements
* Separate supplements from tacrolimus/cyclosporine by at least 2 hours

---

# 💧 Section 03 — Hydration

## Daily Water Target

**8–10 glasses (2–2.5 litres/day)**

Purpose:

* Protect kidneys
* Flush medications
* Support circulation

---

## Recommended

### 🟢 Drink

* Filtered water
* Boiled water
* Herbal teas
* Diluted pasteurized juice

### 🟡 Limit

* Caffeine
* Sports drinks
* Carbonated drinks

### 🔴 Avoid

* Alcohol
* Unpasteurized juices
* Raw herbal tonics

---

## Important Tips

* Never take tacrolimus/cyclosporine with grapefruit juice
* Increase fluids during fever or sweating
* Pale yellow urine = hydrated

# 🏃 Section 04 — Exercise & Physical Activity

## Recovery Exercise Timeline

### Phase 1 — Day +100 to +120

* 5–10 min walks
* Gentle stretching
* Deep breathing
* Seated exercises

### Phase 2 — Day +120 to +180

* Outdoor walking
* Light cycling
* Gentle yoga
* Resistance bands

### Phase 3 — Month 6–9

* Swimming (if cleared)
* Light weights
* Pilates
* Tai chi

### Phase 4 — Month 9–12

* Jogging
* Moderate weights
* Low-contact sports

### Phase 5 — Year 2+

* Full fitness activity
* Hiking
* Running
* Team sports

---

## ⛔ Stop Exercise Immediately If

* Chest pain
* Severe breathlessness
* Dizziness
* Fever > 38°C
* Severe fatigue

---

## 🚫 Avoid Until Cleared

* Contact sports
* Crowded gyms
* Heavy lifting
* Extreme heat
* High altitude

---

## ✅ Best Early Activities

* Quiet walks
* Gentle yoga
* Chair exercises
* Breathing exercises
* Light gardening with gloves

# 🌙 Section 07 — Sleep Optimization

## Sleep Goals

* 8–9 hours nightly
* Same bedtime daily
* Short naps allowed

---

## Ideal Sleep Environment

* Clean bedroom
* Cool room (18–20°C)
* Blackout curtains
* Fresh sheets weekly
* HEPA filter if possible

---

## Good Sleep Habits

* No screens before bed
* No caffeine after 2 PM
* Herbal tea
* Evening breathing/stretching

---

# 🧠 Section 08 — Mental Health & Emotional Wellbeing

## Common Feelings

* Anxiety
* Depression
* Brain fog
* Mood swings
* Isolation
* Identity changes

These experiences are common after SCT recovery.

---

## ✅ Helpful Practices

* Therapy
* Support groups
* Meditation
* Journaling
* Art/music/writing
* Gentle social connection

---

## 🧘 Daily Mind-Body Protocol

* Morning breathing
* Gratitude journaling
* Nature exposure
* One enjoyable activity daily
* Limit doom-scrolling

---

## ⚠️ Seek Help If

* Hopelessness > 2 weeks
* Severe anxiety
* Appetite disappears
* Self-harm thoughts
* Social withdrawal

---

# 🛡️ Section 09 — Infection Prevention

## 🤲 Hand Hygiene

* Wash hands for 20 seconds
* Use sanitizer when needed
* Avoid touching face
* Ask visitors to wash hands

---

## 🏠 Home Safety

* No live plants in bedroom
* Change filters monthly
* Avoid construction dust
* Avoid sick people/crowds

---

## 🚨 Emergency Signs

Call your team immediately if you experience:

* Fever ≥ 38°C
* Chills
* Rash
* Breathing difficulty
* Severe diarrhea/vomiting
* Uncontrolled bleeding

---

# 📋 Section 10 — Weekly Health Checklist

## Weekly Essentials

* Clinic appointments attended
* Medications on time
* Daily hydration maintained
* Balanced meals eaten
* Exercise completed
* Sleep goals achieved
* SPF used outdoors
* No fever episodes
* Social connection maintained

---

## 🔬 Monthly Lab Goals

* Stable CBC
* Proper tacrolimus/cyclosporine levels
* Stable kidney & liver function
* Electrolytes normal
* Vitamin D above 30 ng/mL
* No CMV reactivation
* Stable weight
* GVHD assessment completed

---

# Final Note

You have already done the hardest part.

Recovery after SCT is a gradual rebuilding process — physically, mentally, and emotionally. Protect your immune system, follow your medical team's advice closely, and allow yourself patience during healing.
