# 🏥 Phase 0 — Post-Surgery Rehabilitation

> Medical clearance is mandatory.

## Goals

* Restore mobility
* Reduce pain
* Improve circulation
* Rebuild stability
* Resume normal movement patterns

## Recovery Priorities

* Physiotherapy
* Walking
* Breathing exercises
* Core activation
* Anti-inflammatory nutrition
* 9–10 hours sleep

## Sample Exercises

| Exercise                | Sets           |
| ----------------------- | -------------- |
| Diaphragmatic Breathing | 3 × 10 breaths |
| Ankle Pumps             | 3 × 20         |
| Leg Slides              | 3 × 12         |
| Shoulder Rolls          | 3 × 10         |
| Assisted Walking        | 10–30 min      |
| Isometric Holds         | 3 × 20 sec     |

# 😴 Recovery Rules

1. Sleep 7–10 hours.
2. Train hard, recover harder.
3. Deload every 6–12 weeks.
4. Stay hydrated.
5. Manage stress.
6. Prioritize technique over ego lifting.
7. Never skip warm-ups.

---

# ⚠ Important Warnings

* Get medical clearance after surgery.
* Increase training volume gradually.
* Do not chase weight at the expense of form.
* Avoid overtraining.
* Recovery drives growth.
* Consistency beats intensity.

---

## Golden Rule

> Build health first, strength second, muscle third, aesthetics fourth. Long-term consistency always wins.
